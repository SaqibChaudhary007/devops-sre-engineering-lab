# D00 Interview Bank & Follow-Up Trees

## Interview Levels

- L1 Foundation — define
- L2 Engineer — explain and connect
- L3 Senior — failure and troubleshooting
- L4 SRE — reliability and operations
- L5 Architect — requirements and trade-offs

## Follow-Up Progression

```text
What?
→ Why?
→ How?
→ What fails?
→ How do you know?
→ How do you recover?
→ How do you operate it reliably?
→ How would you redesign it?
→ What trade-off did you make?
```

## Interview Mastery

- IM-1 Recall
- IM-2 Explain
- IM-3 Apply
- IM-4 Defend
- IM-5 Lead

Follow-up depth is tracked from FD-1 through FD-5.

The global [Interview Bank](../../interview-bank/README.md) stores questions, follow-up trees, production interviews, architecture interviews, SRE interviews and mock interviews.
