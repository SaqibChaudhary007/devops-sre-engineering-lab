## Status

- NOT STARTED
- IN PROGRESS
- COMPLETE
- COMPETENT
- MASTERED
- REVIEW REQUIRED
- STALE

## Competent

Recommended minimum:

- knowledge ≥ 80%
- all critical checkpoints passed
- practical ≥ P3
- troubleshooting ≥ 75%
- interview ≥ IM-3
- follow-up ≥ FD-3
- capstone passed

## Mastered

Recommended minimum:

- knowledge ≥ 90%
- no critical area below 80%
- practical ≥ P4
- troubleshooting ≥ 85%
- SRE reasoning ≥ 80%
- architecture ≥ 80%
- interview ≥ IM-4
- follow-up ≥ FD-4
- teach-back ≥ 4/5
- capstone ≥ 85%
- no unresolved critical gaps

Completion is not mastery.
