# D00 Objectives & Learning Outcomes

## Domain Objective

Build a system-level mental model of how software is created, delivered, operated, observed, secured, scaled, failed, recovered and designed.

## L1 — Foundation

Explain computers, operating systems, applications, infrastructure, cloud, DevOps, CI/CD, containers, observability and reliability in plain language.

## L2 — Engineer

Connect source code, builds, artifacts, configuration, infrastructure, automation, delivery and runtime behavior.

## L3 — Senior Engineer

Reason about dependencies, bottlenecks, blast radius, failure modes, evidence, root cause and engineering trade-offs.

## L4 — SRE

Connect technical behavior to user impact, SLI/SLOs, incidents, capacity, toil, recovery and operational risk.

## L5 — Architect

Start from requirements and constraints, compare alternatives and defend decisions across reliability, performance, security, cost, complexity and operability.

## Exit Outcome

The learner should stop asking only “Which tool should I use?” and begin asking “What problem exists, what constraints matter, how can the system fail, and what trade-off am I making?”
