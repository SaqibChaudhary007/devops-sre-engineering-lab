
# D00 — Engineering Foundations & DevOps Mental Models

## Purpose

Build the mental foundation required before deep study of Linux, networking, containers, Kubernetes, cloud, SRE, platform engineering and architecture.

The goal is to move from:

> “I know some DevOps tools.”

to:

> “I understand the systems those tools operate and the engineering problems they solve.”

## Entry Requirements

No Linux, programming, cloud, DevOps or Kubernetes experience is required.

1. Computing & OS foundations
2. Application & infrastructure
3. Cloud, state, scale & resilience
4. DevOps delivery
5. Containers & orchestration
6. Observability, security & production readiness
7. Failure, troubleshooting & incidents
8. Reliability & SRE
9. Distributed systems & architecture
10. Professional engineering maturity
